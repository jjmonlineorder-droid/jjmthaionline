JJM THAI — STEP 1 CUSTOMER WEBSITE STRUCTURE

FILES
- index.html: complete customer-facing page based on the supplied JJM page.
- image-editor.html: local photo selection panel with live customer-page preview.
- assets/: all original supplied photos, posters, logo and graphics.

HOW TO VIEW
1. Extract the ZIP to a folder on your computer.
2. Open image-editor.html in Chrome or Edge.
3. The customer page appears on the right. Click FULL PAGE to open it separately.
4. Choose multiple photos under a product to see them immediately in the customer preview. Click a thumbnail in the customer page to switch the main photo.

IMPORTANT SCOPE
This is STEP 1: customer-facing page structure and local visual/photo preview only.
It is NOT connected to Google Sheets, Apps Script, live checkout, tax, payments, order email or order confirmation yet. The existing page itself clearly labels the preview as not connected. Do not deploy this as a live ordering system yet.

To permanently publish selected photos later, copy the chosen image files into assets/ and update the corresponding image paths. The image editor's temporary uploads are for preview only and are not saved as live website assets.
